Ukulele chord chart

Standard tuning, G C E A. Frets read from the G string to the A string.

Start with four: C, Am, F and G. In the key of C those are the 1, 6m, 4 and 5, and they cover a huge share of pop, folk and worship songs. Add the rest as songs ask for them.

Which chords go together

  • Key of C: C, Dm, Em, F, G, Am
  • Key of G: G, Am, Bm, C, D, Em
  • Key of F: F, Gm, Am, Bb, C, Dm
  • Key of D: D, Em, F#m, G, A, Bm
