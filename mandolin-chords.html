Mandolin chord chart

Standard tuning, G D A E (same as a violin). Frets read from the G pair to the E pair. Each number is one pair of strings.

Start with G, C and D. In the key of G that's the 1, 4 and 5, and it carries a lot of bluegrass, folk and worship. Em is the next one songs ask for. The rest can wait until a song makes you learn them.

These are open and low-position shapes, the friendly ones. Bluegrass players also use closed "chop" shapes higher up the neck for that percussive off-beat sound. Those come later, and your fingertips will let you know when.

Which chords go together

  • Key of G: G, Am, Bm, C, D, Em
  • Key of D: D, Em, F#m, G, A, Bm
  • Key of C: C, Dm, Em, F, G, Am
  • Key of A: A, Bm, C#m, D, E, F#m
