Chord finder

Any chord, any tuning. Same fingering engine as the Chartless app.

Every chord in a key

Shapes are worked out for the strings as they're actually tuned, so DADGAD and open G get real shapes for those tunings instead of standard ones that don't fit. The finder draws the basic major or minor shape; sevenths, sus and add chords show their underlying triad.

Shapes for the song that's playing

The Chartless app runs this same engine live. Play a song from YouTube, your library or the band through the mic, and every chord comes up shaped for your instrument and tuning as it happens.
