Online tuner

Guitar, bass, ukulele, mandolin and banjo. Any tuning on the list. Runs in your browser, nothing uploaded.
–
Tap Start and allow the microphone. Pluck one string at a time.

Green means within 3 cents, which is closer than most ears can tell. Blue is flat, amber is sharp. Tune up to the note rather than down to it, and let each string ring for a second so the reading settles.

Want the full tuner?

The Chartless app does a lot more with the same idea: strum all six strings at once, a strobe mode, thirteen different ways to see in tune, and Magical tuning for when the chords still sound sour with every needle dead center (here's why that happens). Then it'll chart the chords of whatever song is playing, shaped for the tuning you're in.
