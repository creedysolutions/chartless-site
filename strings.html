How the app picks for you

The iPhone app knows your instrument (body style, scale length, tonewoods, or the exact model from a list of about 1,800) and the set that's on it now. After you restring, you strum once into the phone and name that fresh sound. Every check after that measures how far the strings have drifted from it: how much of the top end is gone, how fast the high partials die, how far the harmonics have wandered sharp. That's where "about 34 days left" comes from. Then it hands you the reorder button, and if you want a different sound, the picks above filtered for your instrument. More about the iPhone app.
