Guitar tunings list

Every tuning the Chartless tuner knows, with the notes low to high. Linked ones have their own chord chart or guide.

Drop tunings move the low string and leave the rest alone. Open tunings retune several strings so the guitar rings a chord with nobody fretting anything, which is great right up until you try your usual chord shapes. That's what the linked charts are for.
