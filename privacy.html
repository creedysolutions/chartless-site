Privacy Policy

Chartless — No song left behind · Effective date: August 2026 · Contact: chartless@creedysolutions.com

Chartless is a Google Chrome extension that listens to the audio of the browser tab you choose — or, in Microphone mode, to your own playing — and displays the chords it detects — the current chord, the song's key, recent chords, and optional fingering diagrams — as an overlay on that tab.

Short version: Chartless does not collect any personal data. Tab or microphone audio is analyzed entirely inside your browser, in real time, and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. There are no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

What Chartless processes, and where

  • Tab audio — captured only after you press Start in the extension popup, and only for that tab. The audio stream is analyzed in memory (pitch and rhythm features) and discarded immediately. No audio is ever written to disk, recorded, or sent over the network. Pressing Stop (or closing the tab) ends the capture.
  • Microphone audio (Microphone mode, Pro) — only if you switch the source to Microphone and press Start. Chrome asks for microphone access once; from then on the mic is opened only while you are listening and released on Stop. Same rules as tab audio: analyzed in memory, never recorded, never written to disk, never sent anywhere. Chartless does not play the mic back through your speakers.
  • Page text for the AI-likelihood meter — the overlay reads the current page's own title, description, and channel/artist name (and any platform "AI-generated" label) to guess whether the song was AI-made. That reading happens inside the tab, is used only to draw the meter, and is not stored or transmitted. You can turn the meter off in the popup.
  • Chord sheets you export — the PDF is generated inside your browser from the chords in the ribbon and saved to your Downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Detection results — the chords, key, tempo, and chord-progression patterns detected for a song may be cached locally in your browser's extension storage so the next play of the same song locks in faster. This data never leaves your device.
  • Your settings — mode, presets, instrument choice, overlay position. Stored locally in your browser's extension storage only.

What Chartless does not do

  • No audio recording, storage, or transmission — ever.
  • No collection of personal information of any kind.
  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or third-party services.
  • No accounts and no sign-ins. During listening and chord display the extension makes no network requests at all.
  • No modifying of page content. The overlay is drawn on top of the page. The only page text the extension reads is the song's title/description/channel for the optional AI-likelihood meter, described above — never form fields, never anything you type.

Chartless Pro licensing (the one exception)

If you activate a Chartless Pro license key or start a free trial, the extension makes a single request to our license server (chartless-license.creedysolutions.workers.dev). That request contains exactly two things: your license key (or nothing, for a trial) and a random install identifier generated by the extension. No audio, no song data, no browsing information, no personal details. The server answers with a signed entitlement token that the extension verifies and stores locally. A paid license silently re-validates every few days; everything else about the extension keeps working fully offline.

Permissions, explained

  • tabCapture — to receive the tab's audio stream after you press Start. This is the entire point of the extension.
  • offscreen — Chrome's mechanism for running the audio pipeline in the background while the popup is closed.
  • activeTab + scripting — to draw the chord overlay on the tab you started capture on. Injection happens only on that tab, only after you press Start.
  • storage — to save your settings and per-song detection caches locally.
  • Microphone — not an extension permission; a standard browser microphone prompt you answer once, only if you choose Microphone mode. You can revoke it anytime from Chrome's site settings for the extension.
