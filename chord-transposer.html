Chord transposer

Paste chords or chords over lyrics. Pick the key it's in and the key you want. Lyrics stay put.

  
It handles slash chords (D/F♯), sevenths, sus, add, dim and the rest, and it uses sharps or flats to match the key you're moving to. If a line is mostly words, it's left alone, so "A" in a lyric doesn't turn into "B".

  
Or skip the paperwork

  
Chartless listens to whatever is playing and shows the chords live, and its capo advisor (part of Pro) tells you where to put the capo so the shapes stay easy. Handy when the worship leader moves the song down a whole step at soundcheck.
