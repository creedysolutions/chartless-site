Banjo chord chart

5-string banjo, open G tuning (g D G B D). Frets read from the 4th string (D) to the 1st (D). The short 5th string rings open.

Start with three: G, C and D. In the key of G those are the 1, 4 and 5, and they carry most bluegrass, old-time and folk tunes. Add Em for the 6m and you've got a big share of country and worship songs too.

About that 5th string: it's a high G drone, so it sounds great on G, C, Em and D, and a little tense on A, E and F. Lots of players let it ring anyway, or leave it out of the roll on those chords. When you capo the long strings for a new key, slip the 5th string under a railroad spike at the matching fret so it moves up too.

Which chords go together

  • Key of G: G, Am, Bm, C, D, Em
  • Key of C: C, Dm, Em, F, G, Am
  • Key of D: D, Em, F#m, G, A, Bm
  • Key of A (capo 2, play G shapes): A, Bm, C#m, D, E, F#m

Bluegrass bands love A and B. Rather than learn new shapes, most banjo players capo and keep their G-key shapes. The capo chart shows where, and the transpose chart does the rest.
