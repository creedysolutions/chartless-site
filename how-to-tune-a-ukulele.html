How to tune a ukulele

Soprano, concert and tenor ukulele, standard G C E A tuning.

Standard ukulele tuning is G C E A, starting from the string nearest your chin. Here's the part that surprises guitar players: that G is usually tuned higher than the C next to it. It's called re-entrant tuning, and it's why a uke strum sounds bright and close together instead of deep. So if your tuner app keeps insisting your G is an octave off, it might be right, and so might you.

Tuning it by ear

Get the C string right first, from a piano or any reference. Then:

  • C string, 4th fret is E. Tune the open E string to it.
  • E string, 5th fret is A. Tune the open A string to it.
  • C string, 7th fret is the high G. Tune the open G string to it.

Tune up to the note, never down to it. New uke strings stretch a lot for the first week or so, so retuning every few minutes in that stretch is normal and not a sign the uke is broken.

Low G and baritone

Low G keeps the same notes but drops the G an octave, so the strings run low to high like a guitar. It needs a thicker G string made for it. Baritone ukulele is a different animal: D G B E, like the top four strings of a guitar. There's a baritone chord chart for that.
