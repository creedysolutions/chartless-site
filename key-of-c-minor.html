Chords in the key of C minor

C minor, natural minor. Relative major: E♭.

Same notes and chords as E♭ major, just with Cm as home. Here's the twist minor keys pull: the 5 chord is minor on paper (Gm), but most songs swap in a major G because it pulls home harder. If a minor-key song suddenly sounds dramatic right before the chorus, that's usually the culprit.

The workhorses are the 1m, 4m, ♭6 and ♭7 (Cm, Fm, A♭, B♭), plus that borrowed major 5 (G).

Progressions you'll actually run into

  • 1m ♭6 ♭3 ♭7 (The big minor-key pop and worship sound): Cm · A♭ · E♭ · B♭
  • 1m 4m 5m (Three-chord minor, folk and old ballads): Cm · Fm · Gm
  • 1m ♭7 ♭6 5 (The flamenco walk-down (with a major 5)): Cm · B♭ · A♭ · G
  • 1m 4m 1m 5 (Minor blues-style changes (major 5)): Cm · Fm · Cm · G

Those numbers are the Nashville number system, written from the minor home chord.
